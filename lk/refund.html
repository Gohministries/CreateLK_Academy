Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This policy applies to Premium purchases made through CreateLK English.

1. General Policy

Because Premium access is a digital service that may become available immediately after payment confirmation, Premium purchases are generally non-refundable once access has been activated.

2. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same purchase, contact us as soon as possible with the payment information and transaction details.

After verification, an eligible duplicate payment may be refunded.

3. Failed Payment

If a payment fails, Premium access will not normally be activated.

If your bank or payment provider shows a deduction but the payment was not successfully completed, contact us with the transaction details so the payment status can be checked.

4. Payment Pending

A PayHere transaction may occasionally require additional processing or verification. Premium access may remain unavailable until successful payment confirmation is received.

5. Incorrect Account

If you accidentally make a payment while signed into the wrong account, contact support immediately. We may investigate the transaction and determine whether account correction is possible.

6. Technical Problems

If you have successfully paid but Premium access is not activated because of a technical problem, contact us. We will first attempt to correct the account or activate the purchased access.

7. Refund Requests

Refund requests should include:

  • Your CreateLK account email.
  • Payment date.
  • PayHere transaction/reference information.
  • Amount paid.
  • A description of the issue.

Send requests to:

createlksupport@gmail.com

8. Fraudulent Transactions

Transactions suspected of fraud or unauthorized use may be investigated and may be reported to the relevant payment provider or authorities where appropriate.

9. Cancellation

Unless a recurring subscription is explicitly offered, Premium purchases are one-time purchases for the stated access period and do not require cancellation.

10. Processing

Where a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time may depend on the payment provider and financial institution.

11. Policy Changes

CreateLK may update this Refund Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.
