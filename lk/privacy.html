Payments may be processed using PayHere.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security requirements.

If you choose Google login, authentication is handled through Google's authentication services. We receive only the account information permitted by the authentication process.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide authentication, payment processing, hosting, security or other essential services.

9. Data Retention

We may retain account and quiz information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain records, prevent fraud and meet legal or operational requirements.
