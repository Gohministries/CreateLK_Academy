Payment Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Payment Processing

CreateLK English may use PayHere or another authorized payment provider to process payments for Premium membership.

Payment information is processed through the payment provider according to its security practices and policies.

Important: CreateLK does not intentionally store complete card numbers or CVV/security codes.

2. Premium Activation

Premium access is activated after successful payment confirmation is received from the payment provider.

If a payment is pending, unsuccessful, cancelled, or cannot be verified, Premium access may not be activated until the payment is successfully confirmed.

3. Premium Period

Premium access is provided for the period shown when purchasing the selected Premium plan.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, Premium membership does not automatically renew after the membership period ends.

4. Failed or Pending Payments

If money has been deducted from your account but Premium access has not been activated, please contact us with the relevant payment information so that we can investigate the issue.

5. Duplicate Payments

If you believe that you have accidentally made a duplicate payment, please contact us as soon as possible. We will review the payment records and determine the appropriate resolution.

6. Refunds

Digital Premium access is generally non-refundable once Premium access has been successfully activated.

However, payment issues such as duplicate payments, technical errors, or a payment being deducted without successful Premium activation may be reviewed individually.

Any refund, where applicable, may be subject to the payment provider's procedures and processing requirements.

7. Payment Provider

Payments may be handled by PayHere or another authorized third-party payment provider. The payment provider may have its own terms, privacy policy, refund procedures, and security requirements.

8. Price Changes

CreateLK English may change Premium prices, plans, discounts, or promotional offers at any time. The applicable price will be the price displayed at the time of purchase.

9. Contact

Email: createlksupport@gmail.com

Please provide your payment reference or other relevant transaction details when contacting support.
