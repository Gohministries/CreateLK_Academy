Affiliate Guide

Share CreateLK English • Help students learn • Earn commission

🎯 How the Affiliate Program Works

You share your personal CreateLK English referral link with students, friends or anyone interested in improving their English. When a referred student purchases an eligible Premium membership, you can earn commission according to the current affiliate program.

Simple idea: Share your link → Bring students → Encourage Premium learning → Earn commission from approved sales.

🚀 Step-by-Step

1

Open Your Affiliate Dashboard

Login to CreateLK English and open your Affiliate Dashboard. Your personal referral link will be displayed there.

2

Copy Your Referral Link

Tap Copy Link. This is your personal affiliate link. Always use your own link when promoting CreateLK English.

3

Share With Students

Share your link through WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, personal messages or with people you know who want to improve their English.

4

Create a WhatsApp Group

Create a WhatsApp learning group for interested students. Share your CreateLK English referral link in the group and invite students to join.

5

Encourage Students to Start

Explain that they can begin with the available free lesson and practice English regularly before choosing Premium lessons.

6

Promote Premium Lessons

When students are ready for more learning content, explain the benefits of Premium lessons and show them how to purchase Premium. Never pressure students or make false promises.

7

Check Your Dashboard

Your Affiliate Dashboard shows referrals, approved sales, commission and withdrawal information.

8

Withdraw Your Commission

When your available commission reaches the minimum withdrawal amount shown in your dashboard, you can submit your bank details and withdrawal request.

💬 WhatsApp Promotion Strategy

A simple way to promote CreateLK English is to create a small English-learning WhatsApp group.

  • Invite students who are genuinely interested in English.
  • Share your CreateLK English referral link.
  • Encourage students to practise regularly.
  • Remind them about new lessons and Premium learning content.
  • Answer basic questions and encourage consistent learning.
  • Avoid spam and repeated unwanted messages.
