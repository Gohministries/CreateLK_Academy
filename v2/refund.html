Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to Premium memberships purchased through CreateLK English using online payment or manual bank payment.

2. Premium Membership

CreateLK English Premium provides access to eligible Premium educational content for the membership period stated at the time of purchase.

Premium membership is a digital service. Once Premium access has been activated and the digital content has been made available, refunds are generally not available.

3. Online Payments

Online payments may be processed through PayHere or another authorized payment service. A successful payment does not automatically guarantee a refund.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, please contact us with your payment details so we can investigate the transaction.

4. Manual Bank Payments

For manual bank payments, Premium access is provided only after the payment has been received and verified.

  • Unverified payment claims will not activate Premium access.
  • Incorrect or incomplete payment information may delay verification.
  • If a payment cannot be verified, contact support with your payment proof.

5. Cancellation Before Activation

If you have made a payment but Premium access has not yet been activated, contact us as soon as possible if you want to cancel the purchase. We will review the request and determine whether a refund is applicable.

6. After Premium Activation

Once Premium membership has been activated or Premium content has been accessed, cancellation does not normally result in a refund, except where required by applicable law or where we determine that an error occurred.

7. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you accidentally make a duplicate payment or are charged more than once for the same purchase, contact us. After verification, an appropriate correction or refund may be considered.

8. Technical Problems

If you have paid successfully but cannot access the Premium service because of a technical problem, please contact support. We will first attempt to resolve the access issue or restore the purchased service.

9. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time may depend on the original payment method and the relevant payment provider or bank.

10. How to Request Help

Please contact us as soon as possible and provide your payment confirmation or relevant transaction information.

💬 Contact via WhatsApp ✉️ Email Support

11. Policy Changes

We may update this policy when necessary. The latest version will always be published on this page with the applicable update date.
