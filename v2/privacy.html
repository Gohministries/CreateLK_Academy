Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

CreateLK English respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it and how we protect it when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Name and email address when you sign in with Google.
  • Profile information provided through Google authentication.
  • Learning progress, XP, Coins and quiz activity.
  • Premium membership and payment-related information.
  • Affiliate and referral information.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

3. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is handled through Google. We may receive basic account information such as your name, email address and profile picture to create and manage your CreateLK English account.

We do not receive or store your Google password.

4. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide English lessons and learning features.
  • To save your learning progress and quiz results.
  • To manage Premium membership.
  • To process or verify payments.
  • To operate the affiliate and referral system.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To maintain and improve our services.
  • To help protect the platform from misuse and abuse.

5. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. Payment information may be handled directly by the relevant payment provider according to its own privacy policy and terms.

For manual bank payments, information voluntarily sent to us for payment verification may be retained as necessary to confirm the transaction and provide the purchased service.

6. Cookies & Sessions

CreateLK English may use cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in and maintain a secure session. These technologies help the website recognize your active account.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website, authentication, payments, hosting, security or other essential services.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information from unauthorized access, alteration or misuse. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain accounts, process transactions, meet operational requirements and resolve disputes.

10. Third-Party Services

CreateLK English may use third-party services such as Google authentication, payment providers, hosting infrastructure and communication services. Their use of information is governed by their respective policies.

11. Children's Privacy

CreateLK English is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission where required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised update date.
