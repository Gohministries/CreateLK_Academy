Morning Prayer

Heavenly Father, Thank You for giving me another beautiful day. Guide my thoughts, words and actions today. Give me wisdom to make the right decisions. Help me to walk in Your ways and remain faithful to You. Protect my family and everyone around me. Fill my heart with Your peace, love and joy. In Jesus' name, Amen.
“This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.”

— Psalm 118:24

Prayer for Protection

Heavenly Father, Please protect me, my family and my loved ones. Keep us safe from every danger and evil. Give us strength when we are weak and courage when we are afraid. Let Your presence remain with us throughout this day. In Jesus' name, Amen.
“The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.”

— Psalm 23:1

Prayer for Peace

Lord Jesus, Fill my heart with Your peace. Remove fear, worry and anxiety from my heart. Help me to trust You even when I cannot see the way ahead. Teach me to forgive others and to live in love and kindness. In Jesus' name, Amen.
“And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.”

— Philippians 4:7
