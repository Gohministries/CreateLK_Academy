Premium access provides immediate access to digital educational lessons and learning materials.

If your payment was successfully completed but Premium access was not activated because of a technical problem, please contact us with your payment details. We will investigate the issue and, where appropriate, restore access or provide another reasonable resolution.

If you believe you have accidentally made a duplicate payment for the same Premium access, contact us as soon as possible. We will review the transaction and take appropriate action where a duplicate payment is confirmed.

5. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a payment was made without your authorization, contact us promptly and also contact your payment provider or bank where appropriate.

6. Failed Payments

If a payment fails or is cancelled before successful completion, no Premium access will be provided for that unsuccessful transaction.

Refunds are not provided based on the user's expected or desired results from studying the material. CreateLK Academy does not guarantee monetization approval, followers, reach, views or earnings.

For payment or refund-related questions, please contact CreateLK Academy through our Contact page.
