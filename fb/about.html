About CreateLK Academy

CreateLK Academy is an independent online education platform created to provide practical and accessible learning resources.

The Facebook Creator Study is designed to help learners understand important creator concepts in a simple and structured way.

The study covers areas such as content creation, Reels, creator tools, analytics, audience growth, policies, copyright and monetization awareness.

The learning experience is designed for self-paced study, allowing learners to access the available educational content and continue learning at their own pace.
