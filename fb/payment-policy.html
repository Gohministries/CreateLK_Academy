Premium payments may be processed through PayHere or another payment method made available by CreateLK Academy.

After successful payment confirmation, Premium access will be activated for the applicable access period.

The standard Premium access period is 30 days from activation unless otherwise stated at the time of purchase.

5. Pricing

Prices are displayed in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). CreateLK Academy may change prices for future purchases. The price applicable to your purchase will be the price clearly displayed at the time of payment.

6. Payment Confirmation

A payment may require confirmation from the payment provider before Premium access is activated.

7. Failed or Cancelled Payments

If a transaction fails, is cancelled or is not successfully confirmed, Premium access will not be activated for that transaction.

8. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have made a duplicate payment, please contact us promptly with the relevant transaction information so that we can investigate.

9. Payment Security

Payment card and banking information is processed through the applicable payment provider. Users should review the payment provider's terms and privacy policies for information about payment processing.

10. Refunds

Refund requests are handled according to our Refund Policy.

11. Educational Service

Payments are made for access to an educational digital service. CreateLK Academy does not sell or promise Facebook monetization approval, earnings, followers, reach or other specific results.

12. Contact

For payment questions or transaction-related assistance, please contact CreateLK Academy through our Contact page.
