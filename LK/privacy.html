Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

CreateLK Jobs respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be used.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, account information, job or service advertisement information, payment information, and information submitted when contacting or using the platform.

2. Job and Service Advertisement Information

Information submitted in a job or service advertisement may be displayed publicly, including the advertisement title, description, category, location, image, and contact information provided by the advertiser.

3. Account Information

Account information may be used to provide authentication, manage advertisements, maintain sessions, and provide access to account features.

4. Payment Information

For paid advertisements, payment information submitted for verification may include depositor name, deposit date, reference number, bank information, and payment slip information.

5. How Information Is Used

  • To provide and operate CreateLK Jobs.
  • To manage user accounts.
  • To publish and manage advertisements.
  • To verify payments.
  • To prevent abuse and fraudulent activity.
  • To improve website security and functionality.

6. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties. Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, protect users, or prevent fraud and abuse.

7. Public Information

Users should not submit sensitive personal information in publicly visible job or service advertisements. Information intentionally included in an advertisement may be visible to other website visitors.

Please only include contact and personal information in an advertisement that you are comfortable making publicly available.

8. Cookies and Storage

The website may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, and website functionality.

9. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

10. Third-Party Services

CreateLK Jobs may use third-party services for authentication, hosting, storage, payments, analytics, advertising, or other website functions. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply.

11. Children's Privacy

CreateLK Jobs is intended for users who are legally permitted to use online employment and marketplace services. Users should not provide information that they are not legally permitted to provide.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact CreateLK Jobs through the contact information provided on the website.
