Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

CreateLK Jobs respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, account information, job seeker profile information, CV, advertisement details, location, images, and payment verification information.

2. Job Seeker Profiles

When you create a Job Seeker Profile, information such as your name, position, skills, experience, education, location, contact details, and other information you provide may be stored and, after approval, displayed publicly to help employers discover you.

3. Job Advertisements

Information submitted in job advertisements may be displayed publicly, including job title, description, location, salary, images, and contact information provided by the advertiser.

4. Payment Information

For paid services, payment verification may require information such as payment date, depositor name, reference information, account details, and payment slips. Payment information is used for verification and service administration.

5. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate CreateLK Jobs.
  • Create and manage accounts and profiles.
  • Publish approved job advertisements and profiles.
  • Verify payments and manage paid services.
  • Improve security and website functionality.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.

6. Public Information

Information intentionally submitted for public display may be viewed by website visitors. Users should only publish information they are comfortable making publicly available.

Do not include passwords, financial credentials, identity documents, or other sensitive information in public profiles or advertisements unless specifically required.

7. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to operate the service, process or verify payments, comply with applicable law, protect users, or prevent fraud and abuse.

8. Cookies and Browser Storage

CreateLK Jobs may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies for authentication, sessions, security, preferences, and website functionality.

9. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

10. Third-Party Services

CreateLK Jobs may use third-party services for authentication, hosting, storage, payments, analytics, advertising, and other functions. Those services may have their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

CreateLK Jobs is intended for people legally permitted to use employment and marketplace services. Users should not submit information they are not legally permitted to provide.

12. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact information provided on the CreateLK Jobs website.
