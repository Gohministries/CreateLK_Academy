Ad Rules

Last updated: September 22, 2026

These Ad Rules apply to all job and service advertisements submitted to CreateLK Jobs. By posting an advertisement, you agree to comply with these rules and applicable laws.

1. Genuine Advertisements

Advertisements must relate to genuine jobs, recruitment opportunities, businesses, or services. Advertisers must provide truthful and reasonably accurate information.

2. Accurate Information

  • Use an accurate title and description.
  • Select the correct category.
  • Provide the correct province, district, and city.
  • Provide valid contact information.
  • Clearly state important salary, price, fee, or service conditions.
  • Do not make materially false or misleading claims.

3. Prohibited Advertisements

The following are not permitted:

  • Fraudulent, fake, or deceptive job or service offers.
  • Illegal employment, businesses, products, or services.
  • Requests for passwords, PINs, OTPs, bank credentials, or similar confidential information.
  • Impersonation of another person, company, organization, or authority.
  • Spam or substantially duplicate advertisements.
  • Advertisements unrelated to jobs or services.
  • Unlawful, threatening, abusive, obscene, or exploitative content.
  • Content that infringes another person's intellectual property or other legal rights.

4. Fees and Payments

Advertisers must clearly disclose required fees, prices, deposits, or other payment conditions. Advertisements must not be used to facilitate fraudulent, unauthorized, or deceptive payments.

5. Contact Information

Advertisers must have permission to use the contact details provided in an advertisement. Contact information must not knowingly belong to another person or organization without authorization.

6. Images and Content

Images, logos, descriptions, and other uploaded content must be relevant and must not knowingly infringe copyright, trademark, privacy, or other rights. Do not upload stolen, deceptive, obscene, or unlawful material.

7. Duplicate Advertisements

Do not repeatedly submit the same or substantially identical advertisement. Duplicate or spam advertisements may be rejected, removed, or limited.

8. Categories

Advertisers must select the category that most accurately describes the job or service. CreateLK Jobs may change an incorrect category where appropriate.

9. Location

The selected location must accurately represent where the job or service is offered. Remote and multi-location opportunities should be clearly identified.

10. Free Advertisements

Free advertisements are subject to the limits, duration, and conditions displayed by CreateLK Jobs. Free-ad availability may be changed or restricted.

11. Premium Advertisements

Featured, Urgent, Top, and combined premium options provide additional visibility or placement according to the selected package. Premium status does not exempt an advertisement from these rules.

12. Advertising Period

Standard job advertisements are generally active for 30 days from approval. An advertisement may expire automatically after its applicable period.

13. Review and Moderation

CreateLK Jobs may review advertisements before or after publication. Where appropriate, CreateLK Jobs may approve, reject, edit, suspend, limit, or remove advertisements that violate these rules or applicable requirements.

14. No Guarantee of Publication

Submitting an advertisement does not guarantee approval, publication, visibility, or continued availability. CreateLK Jobs may decline an advertisement where necessary to protect users, the platform, or comply with applicable requirements.

15. Advertiser Responsibility

Advertisers are responsible for the accuracy and legality of their advertisements, recruitment activities, services, communications, transactions, and dealings with other users.

16. User Safety

Users should independently verify an advertiser, employer, job opportunity, service, price, and other important information before sharing personal information, making payments, travelling, attending an interview, or entering into an agreement.

Important: CreateLK Jobs provides a platform for job and service advertisements. CreateLK Jobs does not guarantee the identity, qualifications, conduct, legality, accuracy, quality, or reliability of any advertiser, employer, job, service, or transaction. Users are responsible for making their own checks before proceeding.

17. Reporting

Users who believe an advertisement is fraudulent, unlawful, misleading, or otherwise violates these rules should report it to CreateLK Jobs using the contact information provided on the website.

18. Enforcement

CreateLK Jobs may take reasonable platform actions in response to violations, including removing advertisements, restricting posting privileges, suspending accounts, or taking other appropriate action.

19. Changes to These Rules

CreateLK Jobs may update these Ad Rules from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

20. Acceptance

By submitting an advertisement to CreateLK Jobs, you confirm that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge, that you are authorized to post the advertisement, and that the advertisement complies with these Ad Rules and applicable laws.
