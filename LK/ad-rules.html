Ad Rules

Last updated: September 18, 2026

These Ad Rules apply to all job and service advertisements posted on CreateLK Jobs. By submitting an advertisement, you agree to follow these rules.

1. Genuine Jobs and Services

Advertisements must represent genuine employment, recruitment, business, or service opportunities. Users must provide accurate information about the job or service, advertiser, location, and other important details.

2. Accurate Information

  • Use an accurate advertisement title.
  • Provide a clear description.
  • Select the correct category.
  • Provide the correct province, district, and city.
  • Provide accurate contact information.
  • Do not provide misleading prices, salaries, or service information.

3. Prohibited Advertisements

The following types of advertisements are not permitted:

  • Fake or fraudulent job or service offers.
  • Advertisements intended to scam or deceive users.
  • Illegal employment, businesses, or services.
  • Advertisements requesting passwords, PINs, OTPs, or other confidential credentials.
  • Advertisements pretending to represent another person or organization.
  • Spam or repeated advertisements.
  • Misleading or substantially false information.
  • Advertisements unrelated to jobs or services.
  • Advertisements containing unlawful, abusive, or inappropriate content.

4. Fees and Payments

Do not use advertisements to request suspicious or unauthorized payments from job seekers or customers. Any required fee, price, or payment condition should be clearly disclosed and must comply with applicable law.

5. Contact Information

Advertisers must provide contact information that they are authorized to use. WhatsApp and telephone numbers should belong to the advertiser or an authorized representative.

6. Images

Uploaded images should be relevant to the advertisement. Do not upload copyrighted, stolen, misleading, obscene, or unlawful images.

7. Duplicate Advertisements

Do not repeatedly post the same or substantially identical advertisement. CreateLK Jobs may remove duplicate advertisements.

8. Advertisement Categories

Select the category that most accurately describes your job or service. Incorrect categorization may result in the advertisement being rejected or moved to the appropriate category.

9. Location

Advertisers must select the actual location where the job or service is offered. If the opportunity is remote or available in multiple locations, the advertiser should provide accurate information in the advertisement.

10. Free Advertisements

Free advertisements are subject to the free-ad limits, duration, and conditions displayed by CreateLK Jobs. The availability of free advertisements may be changed or limited.

11. Premium Advertisements

Featured, Urgent, Top, and combined premium options provide additional advertisement visibility or placement according to the selected package. Premium status does not exempt an advertisement from these Ad Rules.

12. Review and Approval

Advertisements may be reviewed before publication. CreateLK Jobs may approve, reject, suspend, edit, or remove an advertisement when it does not comply with these rules or applicable requirements.

13. Rejection

If an advertisement is rejected, the advertiser may be provided with a reason where applicable. Submitting an advertisement does not guarantee publication.

14. Expired Advertisements

Advertisements may automatically expire after the applicable advertisement period. Expired advertisements may no longer be displayed as active opportunities.

15. Advertiser Responsibility

Advertisers are responsible for their advertisements, recruitment practices, services, communications with users, transactions, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

16. User Safety

Job seekers and customers should independently verify advertisers, job opportunities, services, prices, and other information before providing personal information, making payments, travelling, attending interviews, or entering into an agreement.

Important: CreateLK Jobs is a marketplace for job and service advertisements. Users should verify the identity of advertisers and the authenticity of opportunities or services before proceeding with any employment, purchase, payment, or other arrangement.

17. Changes to the Ad Rules

CreateLK Jobs may update these Ad Rules when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.

18. Acceptance

By posting an advertisement on CreateLK Jobs, you confirm that the information provided is accurate and that your advertisement complies with these Ad Rules.
