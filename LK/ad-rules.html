Ad Rules

Last updated: September 18, 2026

These Ad Rules apply to all job advertisements posted on CreateLK Jobs. By submitting an advertisement, you agree to follow these rules.

1. Genuine Job Opportunities

Advertisements must represent genuine employment or recruitment opportunities. Users must provide accurate information about the position, employer, work location, and other important job details.

2. Accurate Information

  • Use an accurate job title.
  • Provide a clear job description.
  • Provide the correct province, district, and city.
  • Provide accurate contact information.
  • Do not provide misleading salary or employment information.

3. Prohibited Advertisements

The following types of advertisements are not permitted:

  • Fake or fraudulent job offers.
  • Advertisements intended to scam job seekers.
  • Illegal employment opportunities.
  • Advertisements requesting passwords, PINs, OTPs, or other confidential credentials.
  • Advertisements pretending to represent another person or organization.
  • Spam or advertisements with misleading information.
  • Advertisements unrelated to employment or recruitment.
  • Advertisements containing unlawful or abusive content.

4. Fees and Payments

Do not ask job seekers to make suspicious payments in order to obtain a job. Any required employment-related fee must be clearly disclosed and must comply with applicable law.

5. Contact Information

Advertisers must provide contact information that they are authorized to use. WhatsApp and telephone numbers should belong to the advertiser or authorized recruitment contact.

6. Images

Uploaded images should be relevant to the job advertisement. Do not upload copyrighted, stolen, misleading, obscene, or unlawful images.

7. Duplicate Advertisements

Do not repeatedly post the same job advertisement. CreateLK Jobs may remove duplicate or substantially identical advertisements.

8. Advertisement Categories

Select the category that most accurately describes the job. Incorrect categorization may result in the advertisement being rejected or moved to the appropriate category.

9. Location

Advertisers must select the actual job location. If the position is remote, select the appropriate remote option where available.

10. Free Advertisements

Free advertisements are subject to the free-ad limits, duration, and conditions displayed by CreateLK Jobs. The availability of free advertisements may be changed or limited.

11. Premium Advertisements

Featured, Urgent, Top, and combined premium options provide additional advertisement visibility or placement according to the selected package. Premium status does not exempt an advertisement from these Ad Rules.

12. Review and Approval

Advertisements may be reviewed before publication. CreateLK Jobs may approve, reject, suspend, edit, or remove an advertisement when it does not comply with these rules or applicable requirements.

13. Rejection

If an advertisement is rejected, the advertiser may be provided with a reason where applicable. Submitting an advertisement does not guarantee publication.

14. Expired Advertisements

Advertisements may automatically expire after the applicable advertisement period. Expired advertisements may no longer be displayed as active job opportunities.

15. Employer Responsibility

Advertisers are responsible for their job advertisements, recruitment practices, communications with applicants, and compliance with applicable employment laws and regulations.

16. Job Seeker Safety

Job seekers should independently verify employers and job opportunities before providing personal information, attending interviews, travelling, or making payments.

Important: CreateLK Jobs is a marketplace for job advertisements. Users should verify the identity of employers and the authenticity of job opportunities before proceeding with any employment arrangement.

17. Changes to the Ad Rules

CreateLK Jobs may update these Ad Rules when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.

18. Acceptance

By posting an advertisement on CreateLK Jobs, you confirm that the information provided is accurate and that your advertisement complies with these Ad Rules.
