Refund Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how payments for paid services, Job Seeker Profiles, and job advertisements on CreateLK Jobs are handled.

1. Job Seeker Profiles

A Job Seeker Profile is currently offered for LKR 500 for 365 days. Payment is subject to verification and admin approval. Once the profile has been approved and the service has been provided, a refund may not be available simply because the user chooses not to use the profile.

2. Paid Advertisements

CreateLK Jobs may offer paid advertisement options such as Featured, Urgent, Top, and combined premium packages. Standard advertisement periods are generally 30 days.

3. Payment Verification

Manual bank payments are not considered approved until reviewed and verified by CreateLK Jobs.

4. Before Approval

Unverified payments may remain pending while payment information is reviewed. If a payment cannot be verified, CreateLK Jobs may request additional information or reject the transaction.

5. Refund Requests

Refund requests should be submitted as soon as possible. Each request will be reviewed based on the circumstances, payment status, and whether the paid service has already been provided.

6. Duplicate Payments

For duplicate payments, contact CreateLK Jobs with the relevant payment references so the transaction can be reviewed.

7. Incorrect Payment Amount

If the payment amount does not match the selected service or package, additional verification may be required before the service is activated.

8. Approved Services

Once a Job Seeker Profile or paid advertisement has been approved and the paid service has been provided, a refund may not be available simply because the user chooses not to use the service.

9. Rejected or Invalid Transactions

Fraudulent, unauthorized, misleading, or otherwise invalid transactions may be investigated and handled according to applicable requirements.

10. Refund Method

Where a refund is approved, the refund method and timing will depend on the circumstances and available payment arrangements.

Please keep your bank deposit slip and payment reference number until your payment has been fully verified.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Refund Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For payment or refund questions, please contact CreateLK Jobs using the contact information provided on the website.
