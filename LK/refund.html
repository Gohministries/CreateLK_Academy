Refund Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how payments for paid job and service advertisements on CreateLK Jobs are handled.

1. Paid Advertisements

CreateLK Jobs may offer paid advertisement options such as Featured, Urgent, Top, and combined premium packages for job and service advertisements.

2. Payment Verification

For manual bank deposits, an advertisement payment is not considered approved until the payment has been reviewed and approved by CreateLK Jobs.

3. Before Approval

If a payment has been submitted but has not yet been verified, the advertisement may remain pending while the payment information is reviewed.

4. Rejected Payments

If submitted payment information cannot be verified, CreateLK Jobs may reject the payment and request additional information or clarification.

5. Refund Requests

Refund requests should be made as soon as possible after the payment issue occurs. Each request may be reviewed based on the circumstances of the payment and advertisement.

6. Duplicate Payments

If a user accidentally makes a duplicate payment for the same advertisement, the user should contact CreateLK Jobs with the relevant payment references so the matter can be reviewed.

7. Incorrect Payment Amount

If the amount deposited does not match the selected advertisement package, the payment may require additional verification before the advertisement can be approved.

8. Advertisement Removal

Once a paid advertisement has been approved and its premium service has been provided, refunds may not be available simply because the advertiser later decides not to use the advertisement.

9. Fraudulent or Invalid Transactions

Payments associated with fraudulent, unauthorized, misleading, or otherwise invalid transactions may be investigated and handled according to applicable requirements.

10. Refund Method

Where a refund is approved, the refund method and timing will depend on the circumstances and available payment arrangements.

Please keep your bank deposit slip and payment reference number until your payment has been fully verified.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Refund Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For payment or refund questions, please contact CreateLK Jobs using the contact information provided on the website.
