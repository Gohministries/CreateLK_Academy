Refund Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how payments for paid job and service advertisements on CreateLK Jobs are handled.

1. Paid Advertisements

CreateLK Jobs may offer paid options such as Featured, Urgent, Top, and combined premium packages.

2. Payment Verification

Manual bank payments are not considered approved until reviewed and approved by CreateLK Jobs.

3. Before Approval

Unverified payments may remain pending while payment information is reviewed.

4. Rejected Payments

If payment information cannot be verified, CreateLK Jobs may reject the payment and request additional information.

5. Refund Requests

Refund requests should be made as soon as possible. Each request will be reviewed based on the circumstances.

6. Duplicate Payments

For duplicate payments, contact CreateLK Jobs with the relevant payment references for review.

7. Incorrect Payment Amount

If the payment amount does not match the selected package, additional verification may be required.

8. Advertisement Removal

Once a paid advertisement has been approved and its premium service provided, a refund may not be available simply because the advertiser chooses not to use it.

9. Fraudulent or Invalid Transactions

Fraudulent, unauthorized, misleading, or otherwise invalid transactions may be investigated and handled according to applicable requirements.

10. Refund Method

Where a refund is approved, the method and timing will depend on the circumstances and available payment arrangements.

Please keep your bank deposit slip and payment reference number until your payment has been fully verified.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Refund Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For payment or refund questions, please contact CreateLK Jobs using the contact information provided on the website.
