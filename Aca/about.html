About CreateLK Academy

CreateLK Academy is a structured online Spoken English course built around practical conversation practice. The course contains 100 lessons with 25 dialogues in each lesson, providing a total of 2,500 dialogues.

The learning experience is designed to make English practice simple, organised and consistent. Learners can progress through the lessons, practise conversations and track their learning journey.

The course also provides English, Sinhala and Tamil support, helping learners understand the learning material more easily while developing their spoken English confidence.

A Simple Learning Journey

CreateLK Academy is designed around a simple progression that encourages learners to practise consistently.

  • Create your learner account.
  • Start with Lesson 1, which is always free.
  • Practise the dialogues included in each lesson.
  • Continue through the structured 100-lesson course.
  • Track your learning progress.
  • Complete the full course and work towards your completion certificate.
