How Can We Help?

You can contact CreateLK Academy if you need assistance with any part of your learning experience.

  • Questions about the 100-lesson Spoken English course.
  • Help with accessing or using your learner account.
  • Questions about lessons and dialogue practice.
  • Questions about English, Sinhala or Tamil language support.
  • Help with tracking your course progress.
  • General questions about CreateLK Academy.

Before You Contact Us

CreateLK Academy provides a structured Spoken English course containing 100 lessons and 2,500 dialogues, with 25 dialogues in each lesson.

Lesson 1 is always free, allowing learners to experience the course before continuing with the complete learning journey.

The course provides English, Sinhala and Tamil support and includes progress tracking and a completion certificate.
