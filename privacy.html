Last updated: August 24, 2026

CreateLK Academy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our website and English learning services.

When you create an account, we may collect:

Your information may be used to:

Our services may use third-party providers for hosting, payments, analytics, communication, or other technical services. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Our service is intended for general English learning. Users should provide information only with the appropriate permission when required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We may retain account, membership, payment, and learning information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain records.

9. Your Choices

You may contact us regarding your account information or request correction of inaccurate information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
