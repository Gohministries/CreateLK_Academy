🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

CreateLK Academy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website and 100-Day Tamil → English Spoken English learning service.

1. Information We Collect

When you create or use a CreateLK Academy account, we may collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Account and login information
  • Student account identification information
  • Learning progress and completed lessons
  • Spoken English course activity
  • Premium membership and subscription information
  • Payment or payment-verification information when required

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your student account
  • Provide the 100-Day Spoken English course
  • Provide Tamil → English speaking practice
  • Save and display your learning progress
  • Determine access to free and Premium lessons
  • Manage Premium membership
  • Verify payments and membership requests
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the service
  • Improve our learning platform and services

3. Free and Premium Course Access

CreateLK Academy may provide a free introductory lesson or lessons. Premium lessons may require an active Premium membership. Your membership status may be stored with your student account so that the system can determine which course content you are permitted to access.

4. Learning Progress

CreateLK Academy may store information about lessons you have completed. This information is associated with your student account and is used to show your course progress and allow you to continue your learning journey.

5. Payment Information

Premium membership payments may be processed through third-party payment providers or through payment verification methods provided by CreateLK Academy.

CreateLK Academy does not intentionally store complete payment-card numbers, CVV codes, passwords for banking services, or other complete financial credentials on the learning platform. Payment providers may process payment information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Payment Verification

Where payment verification is required, we may receive or store information necessary to confirm a payment, such as payment reference information, transaction details, or a payment receipt or proof of payment submitted by the student.

7. Account Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect student information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website, application, database, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Students are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use cookies, browser local storage, session storage, or similar technologies to maintain login information, remember preferences, store temporary account information, and provide essential website functionality.

9. Cloud Services and Third-Party Providers

CreateLK Academy may use third-party technology providers for hosting, databases, payment processing, file storage, security, communication, analytics, or other technical services.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services or as otherwise permitted by their applicable terms and policies.

10. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the platform, process payments, provide technical services, protect the service, or comply with legal requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

CreateLK Academy is an English-learning service intended for general educational use. Users should provide personal information only when they are legally permitted to do so. Where applicable law requires parental or guardian permission for a child to use an online service, that permission should be obtained.

12. Data Retention

We may retain account, learning-progress, membership, payment- verification, and related records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain accurate records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, protect the platform, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

13. Account Information and Requests

You may contact CreateLK Academy if you believe your account information is inaccurate or if you have a question about the information associated with your account. Requests will be handled subject to applicable legal requirements and reasonable verification of account ownership.

14. Data Deletion

You may contact us to request deletion of your account or personal information where applicable. Some information may need to be retained when required for legal, security, financial, fraud prevention, dispute-resolution, or record-keeping purposes.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
