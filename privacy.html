Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

CreateLK Academy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, how we use it, and how you can contact us about your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use CreateLK Academy, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, Google account identifier, and profile picture when you sign in using Google.
  • Learning information: Your selected answers, submitted answers, quiz activity, and learning progress.
  • Membership information: Your membership status, premium activation and expiry information, and relevant payment reference or transaction status details.
  • Technical information: Information that your browser or device may provide when accessing the website, such as browser type and basic technical information.

2. Google Sign-In

CreateLK Academy allows users to sign in using Google. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may share information permitted by the authorization process, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier.

Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy. CreateLK Academy does not control Google's privacy practices.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Academy account.
  • Provide access to English grammar lessons and practice questions.
  • Record submitted answers and calculate learning progress.
  • Manage free and premium membership access.
  • Verify payment notifications and update membership status.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service information.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Academy website.

4. Payments and PayHere

Premium membership payments are processed through PayHere. When you make a payment, you may be redirected to PayHere's payment platform.

Payment card and other sensitive payment details are handled by the payment provider. CreateLK Academy does not intend to collect or store your full payment card details.

We may receive or retain relevant transaction information, such as a payment reference, amount, payment status, and details needed to verify and manage your membership.

PayHere processes payment information according to its own privacy and security policies.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website and its technical services may use browser storage technologies, including local storage or session storage, to support website functionality.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Maintain sign-in and session-related information.
  • Remember relevant website settings or activity.
  • Support the payment process and return flow.
  • Maintain parts of your learning experience.

You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings. Clearing stored information may affect sign-in or website functionality.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the Academy, including:

  • Google: For Google Sign-In and account authentication.
  • PayHere: For payment processing.
  • Cloudflare: For website hosting, application services, and related technical infrastructure.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and rights of the Academy and its users.

7. Data Retention

We retain account, learning, membership, and transaction-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it is retained.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through CreateLK Academy. However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should keep their Google account credentials secure and avoid sharing access to their accounts.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your Academy account, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

You may also stop using the Academy and manage your Google account permissions through your Google account settings.

10. Children's Privacy

CreateLK Academy provides educational English grammar practice. If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, they may contact us so that we can review the request.

11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party services, including Google and PayHere. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
