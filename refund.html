💳 Refund Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests are handled for CreateLK Academy Premium membership and digital English learning content.

Please review the Premium membership details, course features and pricing before making a payment.

1. Premium Membership

CreateLK Academy Premium membership currently costs LKR 1,500 for 30 days, unless a different price is clearly displayed on the website at the time of purchase.

Premium members may receive access to additional English learning content, including the 100-Day Spoken English course, Tamil-to-English speaking practice, lessons and other Premium learning features available during the membership period.

2. Digital Educational Content

CreateLK Academy provides digital educational content that may become available immediately after Premium membership is activated. Because the service consists of digital learning content, refund requests may be reviewed individually.

A refund is not automatically guaranteed after Premium content has been accessed, viewed, completed or otherwise used.

3. Technical or Activation Problems

If you have completed a payment but your Premium membership has not been activated because of a technical problem, please contact us as soon as possible.

We will verify the payment and account information and, where appropriate, correct the membership status or provide another reasonable solution.

4. Duplicate Payments

If you accidentally make the same payment more than once for the same membership period, contact us with the relevant payment information.

After verification, a duplicate payment may be eligible for a refund or adjustment.

5. Unauthorized Payments

If you believe a payment was made without your authorization, contact CreateLK Academy as soon as possible and provide the transaction information required to investigate the matter.

6. Refund Requests

Refund requests can be submitted through WhatsApp: +94 77 119 0395.

Please provide your registered account details, payment date, payment amount and payment reference or receipt, if available.

7. Refund Review

Refund requests may be reviewed based on the payment record, account status, Premium activation status, course usage and the circumstances of the request.

If a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time may depend on the payment provider or payment method used.

8. Situations Where a Refund May Not Be Available

A refund may not be available where Premium content has already been substantially accessed or used, where the request is based only on failure to use the membership, where account credentials were shared or misused, or where the service was used in violation of the CreateLK Academy Terms & Conditions.

9. Membership Expiry

Premium membership is provided for the membership period shown at the time of purchase. Expiration of the membership period does not automatically qualify for a refund.

10. Policy Changes

CreateLK Academy may update this Refund Policy when necessary. Changes will be published on this page with the updated date.

11. Contact

For refund, payment or Premium membership support, contact CreateLK Academy through WhatsApp: +94 77 119 0395.
