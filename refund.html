Contact CreateLK Academy

Need help with your account, the 100-Lesson Spoken English Course, Premium membership, payment verification, or a technical problem? Please contact us using the information below.

1. Course & Account Support

Account or Course Help

For account access, lesson progress, Premium membership, or other course-related questions, please contact CreateLK Academy support.

2. Payment Support

Payment Verification

If you have completed a payment but your Premium access has not been activated, please provide your registered email address, payment date, payment amount, and relevant transaction information.

3. WhatsApp Support

For faster assistance, you may contact CreateLK Academy through the official WhatsApp support channel provided on the website.

4. Email Support

Email

Please use the official support email address displayed on the CreateLK Academy website.

5. What to Include in a Support Request

  • Your registered account email address.
  • The lesson or feature you are having trouble with.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • For payment issues, include the payment date and amount.
  • For technical problems, include a screenshot when helpful.

6. Course Information

CreateLK Academy offers a 100-Lesson Spoken English Course containing 2,500 dialogues, with 25 dialogues in each lesson. Lesson 1 is available free, while Premium membership provides access to additional lessons according to the membership plan.

7. Support Response

We aim to review support requests and payment-related issues as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the type of request and verification required.
