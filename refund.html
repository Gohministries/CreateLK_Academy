Refund Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Premium Membership

CreateLK Academy provides digital educational content and daily spoken English practice. Premium membership provides access to additional digital lessons for the membership period.

2. Refund Eligibility

Because Premium membership provides access to digital content, payments are generally non-refundable once Premium access has been activated.

3. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you accidentally make a duplicate payment or pay an incorrect amount, please contact CreateLK Academy as soon as possible. We may review the payment and determine whether a refund or other resolution is appropriate.

4. Failed Payments

If a payment is successfully deducted from your account but Premium membership is not activated because of a payment processing or technical problem, please contact us with your payment details. We will investigate the issue.

5. Payment Verification

Manual bank payments are subject to verification. Uploading a payment slip does not automatically guarantee Premium activation. Premium access is activated after the payment has been successfully reviewed and approved.

6. Contact

For payment or refund questions, please contact CreateLK Academy through the contact information provided on our website.
