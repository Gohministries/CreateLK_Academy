Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Thank you for choosing CreateLK Academy. Please read this Refund and Cancellation Policy before purchasing a Premium membership.

Premium Membership:
LKR 999 for 30 days of Premium access. Membership is a one-time payment and does not automatically renew.

1. Premium Membership

CreateLK Academy offers free and paid Premium learning access. Premium membership provides access to eligible premium English grammar categories and practice questions for the purchased period.

Your 30-day membership period begins when your Premium access is activated.

2. Refund Requests

If you experience a payment problem, duplicate payment, incorrect charge, or another issue relating to your purchase, contact our support team as soon as possible.

We will review refund requests individually, considering the payment record, membership activation status, and applicable consumer protection requirements.

Refunds may be considered in situations such as:

  • A verified duplicate payment for the same membership.
  • A successful payment where Premium access was not provided and the issue cannot reasonably be resolved.
  • An incorrect or unauthorized charge, subject to verification.
  • Other circumstances where a refund is required by applicable law.

3. When Refunds May Not Be Available

A refund may not be available simply because a user changes their mind, does not use the membership, or does not complete the available lessons.

However, nothing in this policy limits any refund or other rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

4. Cancellation and Automatic Renewal

Premium membership is purchased for a fixed 30-day period. It does not automatically renew, and no recurring subscription charge is intended.

You do not need to cancel an automatic renewal. When your membership expires, Premium access will end unless you make another purchase.

5. Payment Verification

Payments are processed through PayHere. Premium access is activated after the payment has been successfully verified by the Academy.

If your payment is successful but your Premium membership has not been activated, please contact us with your registered email address and payment reference or receipt.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be handled through the relevant payment provider or payment method, subject to its procedures and processing timelines.

The time required for a refund to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider and your bank.

7. How to Request a Refund

Contact our support team and provide the following details:

  • Your name.
  • Your registered Academy email address.
  • Payment reference or transaction ID.
  • Payment date and amount.
  • A brief explanation of the issue.
