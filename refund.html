💳 Refund Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances in which a payment for CreateLK Academy Premium membership may be eligible for a refund.

Please review the Premium features and membership terms before making a payment.

1. Premium Membership

Premium membership currently costs LKR 1,500 for 30 days, unless a different price is clearly displayed on the website at the time of purchase.

2. Refund Requests

Because Premium membership provides access to digital educational content, refund requests are considered individually. A refund is not automatically guaranteed after Premium content has been accessed or used.

3. Payment Problems

If you were charged but Premium access was not activated because of a technical or payment-processing problem, please contact us as soon as possible so that we can investigate the transaction.

4. Duplicate Payments

If you accidentally make the same payment more than once for the same membership period, contact us with the relevant payment details. After verification, duplicate payments may be eligible for a refund or adjustment.

5. Unauthorized Payments

If you believe a payment was made without your authorization, contact us immediately and provide the transaction information needed to investigate the issue.

6. How to Request a Refund

Send a refund request through WhatsApp: +94 77 119 0395.

Please include your registered email address, phone number, payment date, amount, and payment reference if available.

7. Refund Review

We may review account activity and payment information before making a refund decision. If a refund is approved, the method and processing time may depend on the payment provider.

8. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not be available where Premium content has been substantially used, where the request results from failure to protect account credentials, or where the service was used in violation of our Terms & Conditions.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy when necessary. The latest version will always be available on this page.

10. Contact

For refund or payment support, contact CreateLK Academy through WhatsApp: +94 77 119 0395.
