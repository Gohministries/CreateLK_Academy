Refund Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. About This Policy

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which payments for CreateLK Academy Premium membership may be refunded.

2. Premium Membership

Premium membership provides access to additional content in the 100-Lesson Spoken English Course, including Lessons 2–100, according to the membership plan purchased.

The current Premium membership period is 30 days unless otherwise stated at the time of purchase.

3. Digital Content

Because Premium membership provides access to digital educational content, refunds are generally not available after Premium access has been successfully activated and the user has received access to the Premium course content.

4. Duplicate Payments

If a user accidentally makes the same payment more than once for the same Premium membership period, the duplicate payment may be eligible for a refund after verification.

5. Payment Made but Access Not Activated

If payment has been successfully completed but Premium access has not been activated due to a technical or verification problem on our side, the user should contact CreateLK Academy support with the payment details and registered account email.

We will review the payment and account information and, where appropriate, activate the Premium membership or provide a suitable resolution.

6. Incorrect or Fraudulent Payment Information

Payments submitted with fraudulent, altered, misleading, or invalid payment information are not eligible for a refund. CreateLK Academy may suspend or restrict the related account where necessary.

7. Technical Problems

If a significant technical problem prevents a user from accessing Premium content after a verified payment, the user should contact support as soon as possible. We will investigate the issue and attempt to restore access or provide an appropriate resolution.

8. Refund Review

Refund requests are reviewed individually. Approval of a refund does not create an automatic right to refunds for other purchases or circumstances.

9. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or report a payment problem, contact CreateLK Academy through the contact information provided on our website. Include your registered email address, payment date, payment amount, and relevant payment or transaction information.

10. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time may depend on the original payment method and the relevant payment provider or financial institution.

11. Changes to This Policy

CreateLK Academy may update this Refund Policy when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

For questions regarding refunds or payment issues, please contact CreateLK Academy through the contact information provided on our website.
