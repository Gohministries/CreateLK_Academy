Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

This Refund Policy explains the refund terms applicable to Premium Membership purchases made through CreateLK Academy.

1. Premium Membership

Premium Membership:

LKR 499 / 30 Days

Premium Membership provides access to the full available daily learning content during the applicable membership period.

2. Refund Eligibility

Because Premium Membership provides immediate access to digital educational content, payments are generally non-refundable once Premium access has been activated.

However, a refund may be considered in exceptional circumstances, such as:

  • A duplicate payment was made for the same membership.
  • Payment was successfully completed but Premium Membership was not activated because of a verified technical or processing problem.
  • An incorrect charge occurred due to a payment-processing error.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will generally not be provided for:

  • Change of mind after purchasing Premium Membership.
  • Failure to use the service after purchasing it.
  • Failure to complete lessons or learning activities.
  • Loss of interest in the service.
  • Failure to remember login credentials.
  • Temporary internet, device or network problems on the user's side.
  • Requests made after Premium content has been accessed, except where required by applicable law or where CreateLK Academy determines an exception is appropriate.

4. Payment Verification Issues

If you have completed a payment but your Premium Membership has not been activated, please contact CreateLK Academy with the relevant payment information so that the transaction can be checked.

Where a payment is successfully verified and the problem is caused by a technical issue within the service, we may activate the membership or provide another appropriate resolution instead of issuing a refund.

5. Duplicate Payments

If you accidentally make more than one successful payment for the same Premium Membership, please contact us as soon as possible.

After verification, an eligible duplicate payment may be refunded using the applicable payment method or another agreed method.

6. Refund Requests

Refund requests should include enough information for us to identify and verify the transaction, such as:

  • Name used for the CreateLK Academy account.
  • Student or account ID, if available.
  • Date of payment.
  • Amount paid.
  • Payment reference or transaction information.

Do not send your password or other confidential login information when requesting a refund.

7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed within a reasonable period after the payment and refund eligibility have been verified.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear may also depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

8. Technical Problems

If you experience a technical problem preventing you from accessing Premium content, please contact us before requesting a refund. We will first make reasonable efforts to resolve the issue.

9. Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a payment was made without your authorization, please contact the relevant payment provider and CreateLK Academy as soon as possible so the transaction can be investigated.

10. Changes to This Policy

CreateLK Academy may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The latest version published on this page will apply to future purchases and requests, subject to applicable law.

11. Contact

For questions about payments, Premium Membership or refunds, please contact CreateLK Academy using the contact information provided on our website.

12. Acceptance

By purchasing a Premium Membership, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Refund Policy.
