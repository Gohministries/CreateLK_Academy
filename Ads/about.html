About CreateLK Academy

Learn English. Build Confidence. Speak Naturally.

Who We Are

CreateLK Academy is an online educational platform focused on making practical English learning accessible to everyone.

Our goal is to help learners improve their everyday spoken English through simple, practical conversations that can be used in real-life situations.

Our mission

To make practical English learning simple, accessible, and completely free for learners everywhere.

Our Spoken English Course

CreateLK Academy currently provides a free online Spoken English Course designed around practical everyday communication.

100 Lessons
2,500 Practical Dialogues
3 Languages

Three Languages

The course is designed to support learners using:

  • English
  • Sinhala
  • Tamil

This multilingual approach helps learners understand practical English conversations more easily while developing confidence in speaking.

100% Free

The Spoken English Course is completely free. There is no subscription, Premium membership, course payment, or login requirement for accessing the lessons.

Supported by Advertising

To help keep the course available without charging learners, the website may display advertisements from third-party advertising networks.

Advertising allows us to provide educational content while keeping access free for users.

Learning Progress

The course can maintain lesson completion progress using an anonymous browser identifier. This allows learners to continue tracking completed lessons without requiring a traditional account or login.

Our Vision

We want to build a useful and accessible learning platform where anyone can practice practical English regularly and develop greater confidence in everyday communication.
