Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how refunds and payment-related requests are handled for purchases made through CreateLK Pro.

1. Digital Service

CreateLK Pro provides digital products and services, including access to premium AI prompts through Coins. Because the service provides digital content, users should review the selected Coin package and price before completing a payment.

2. Coin Purchases

Coins purchased through CreateLK Pro are intended for use within the platform and have no cash value outside the service.

Once Coins have been successfully credited to an account, they may be used to access eligible premium prompts and features.

3. Refund Requests

If you believe that a payment was processed incorrectly, the wrong amount was charged, or a payment was completed but the purchased Coins were not credited, please contact our support team.

Refund requests are reviewed individually based on the transaction, payment status and circumstances of the request, subject to applicable law and the rules of the payment provider.

4. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same transaction or that an incorrect amount was charged, please contact us with your payment or transaction reference.

We will review the transaction and, where appropriate, take corrective action.

5. Payment Completed but Coins Not Received

If your payment was successful but your Coins have not appeared in your CreateLK Pro account, please contact support and provide the relevant payment or transaction reference.

We will verify the payment status and investigate the issue.

6. Cancellation

Coin purchases are generally processed immediately through the payment system. For this reason, cancellation may not be possible after a transaction has been completed.

If you have an issue with a transaction, contact us as soon as possible so that the transaction can be reviewed.

7. Non-Refundable Situations

Subject to applicable law, refunds may not be available where Coins have already been successfully delivered and used to access premium digital content.

Refunds may also not be available for dissatisfaction with an AI-generated result or because a prompt did not produce a particular result in a third-party AI system.

8. Payment Provider

Payments may be processed through a third-party payment provider. Payment processing may also be subject to the provider's applicable terms and conditions.

9. How to Request Support

For payment, refund or transaction-related questions, contact:

createlksupport@gmail.com

Please include your payment or transaction reference, the email address associated with your CreateLK Pro account, the date of the transaction, and a description of the issue.

10. Processing of Requests

Refund and payment requests will be reviewed after the relevant transaction details have been received. Additional information may be requested when necessary to verify the transaction.

11. Policy Changes

This Refund & Cancellation Policy may be updated when necessary. The current version will always be published on this page with the applicable updated date.

12. Contact

For assistance with a refund, cancellation or payment issue, please contact our support team.
