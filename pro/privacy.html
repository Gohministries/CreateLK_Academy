Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

CreateLK Pro respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information used to provide and maintain our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use CreateLK Pro, we may collect information necessary to provide the service, including account information associated with your sign-in, service activity, transaction information and technical information required for security and operation.

CreateLK Pro uses Google Sign-In for authentication. When you sign in, information provided by the authentication service may be used to create and maintain your CreateLK Pro account.

3. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through our payment provider. CreateLK Pro does not require you to provide payment card details directly to the prompt library. Payment-related information may be received or stored as necessary to identify and verify transactions.

Information may be used to:

  • Manage Coins and transactions.

  • Respond to support requests.

  • Improve service reliability and functionality.

5. Cookies and Sessions

CreateLK Pro may use secure session cookies or similar technologies to keep users authenticated and maintain secure access to the service.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate authentication, payments, hosting, security, communications or other essential parts of the service, subject to applicable requirements.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Choices

You may contact CreateLK Pro support if you have questions about your account information or privacy-related requests.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page with the applicable updated date.

11. Contact
