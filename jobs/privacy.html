Privacy Policy

How CreateLK Jobs collects, uses, and protects information.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

CreateLK Jobs respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the CreateLK Jobs platform.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and email address.
  • Profile and account information.
  • Company and employer information.
  • Job advertisements and application information.
  • CVs, cover letters, and other information submitted by users.
  • Payment and transaction-related information where applicable.
  • Technical information such as browser, device, and basic usage information.

3. Google Sign-In

CreateLK Jobs may allow users to sign in using Google. When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as your name, email address, and profile information, according to the permissions and settings applicable to the service.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide job search and recruitment services.
  • Allow employers to publish job advertisements.
  • Allow job seekers to submit applications.
  • Process and verify applicable payments.
  • Improve the platform and user experience.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Communicate important service-related information.

5. Job Applications

When a job seeker applies for a position, information submitted as part of the application may be shared with the relevant employer for recruitment purposes.

6. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. CreateLK Jobs does not intend to store full payment card details when payment processing is handled by an external payment provider.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

CreateLK Jobs may use cookies, local storage, sessions, or similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences, improve security, and provide platform functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for authentication, hosting, payments, analytics, security, or other platform functions. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Information Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the platform.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the available features, you may be able to update account information, manage your profile, or request assistance regarding information associated with your account.

12. Children's Privacy

CreateLK Jobs is intended for people seeking or providing employment opportunities. Users should not provide personal information belonging to children through the platform.

13. External Links

CreateLK Jobs may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact CreateLK Jobs through the contact information provided on our website.
