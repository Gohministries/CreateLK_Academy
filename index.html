Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting.

What is Daily Spoken English?

CreateLK Academy provides a new spoken English lesson every day with practical Tamil → Sinhala → English conversations designed for everyday communication.

How many conversations are available each day?

Each daily lesson contains up to 10 practical conversations. Free members can practice the first 3 conversations, while Premium members can access all 10.

Are the lessons available every day?

Yes. A new daily lesson can be provided each day so you can continue practicing English regularly.

Is it free to use?

Yes. You can access the first 3 conversations of the daily lesson for free.

How much is Premium?

Premium membership costs only LKR 499 for 30 days and unlocks all 10 conversations in the daily lesson.

What languages are used?

The lessons use Tamil, Sinhala and English to help learners understand practical English expressions more easily.

Can I listen to the English sentences?

Yes. The lesson includes a Listen option that uses your device's available speech functionality to read the English sentence aloud.

Can I use CreateLK Academy on my phone?

Yes. CreateLK Academy is designed for smartphones, tablets and computers.

Can I continue learning every month?

Yes. Premium membership is valid for 30 days. You can renew your membership to continue accessing the full daily lessons.
