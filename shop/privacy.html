Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

CreateLK Shop respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website and place an order.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, we may collect your name, phone number, email address if provided, delivery address and order information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to process orders, arrange delivery, communicate with customers, provide customer support and help protect the website and our services.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Service Providers

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to complete an order, delivery, payment or other requested service. Only information reasonably required for the relevant service should be provided.

5. Browser Storage

CreateLK Shop may use browser storage such as localStorage to keep shopping-cart information on your device. This allows your selected products to remain in your cart while you browse the website.

6. Cookies and Technical Data

The website or its hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address and server logs for security, reliability and normal website operation.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the shop. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Order and customer information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for order management, customer support, accounting, security and legitimate business purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The shop is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

If you have a privacy or shop-related question, contact CreateLK Shop through WhatsApp.
