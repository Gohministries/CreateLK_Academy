Who Can Share?

Anyone can share CreateLK Shop products. You do not need to create a customer account just to share a product.

Affiliate earnings are tracked separately from the normal CreateLK Shop customer ordering system.

Commission & Withdrawal

  • Commission rate: 10% of the eligible referred order amount.
  • Minimum withdrawal amount: LKR 1,000.
  • Commission must be successfully recorded in the affiliate system before it becomes available for withdrawal.
  • Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed through the affiliate system.
  • Orders that are cancelled, refunded, returned, fraudulent, or otherwise excluded under the affiliate terms may not qualify for commission.
Important: Commission is not earned simply by clicking or sharing a link. A qualifying purchase must be completed through the applicable referral link and meet the affiliate program requirements.
