Refund & Return Policy

CreateLK Shop

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Thank you for shopping with CreateLK Shop. We want every customer to have a smooth and reliable shopping experience. This policy explains when returns, replacements, cancellations, and refunds may be available.

1. Return Eligibility

Customers may request a return when an item is received damaged, defective, incorrect, or significantly different from the product description.

Return requests should normally be submitted within 7 days of receiving the order.

2. Damaged Products

If your product arrives damaged, please contact us as soon as possible and provide clear photographs or videos showing:

  • The damaged product
  • The packaging or parcel
  • The shipping label, if available
  • Your order or payment reference

After reviewing the information, CreateLK Shop may offer a replacement, refund, or another appropriate solution.

3. Wrong Product

If you receive a product that is different from the item you ordered, please contact us within 7 days of delivery.

After verification, we may arrange a replacement or refund depending on the circumstances.

4. Defective Products

If a product is defective or does not function as described, please contact us with details of the problem and supporting photos or videos where applicable.

The available solution may include repair, replacement, store credit, or refund depending on the product and circumstances.

5. Change of Mind

Returns for simply changing your mind may not be available. Eligibility may depend on the individual product, its condition, and the seller's stated return conditions.

Important: Products must generally be returned in unused condition with their original packaging, accessories, and applicable documentation when a return is approved.

6. Items That Cannot Be Returned

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of their nature, hygiene requirements, personalization, digital delivery, or other applicable restrictions.

Any product-specific return restrictions will be communicated where applicable.

7. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation before an order has been processed or dispatched.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible and the customer may need to follow the applicable return procedure.

8. Refunds

If a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time will depend on the original payment method and applicable payment arrangements.

CreateLK Shop will communicate the applicable refund details after the request has been reviewed.

9. Refund Processing

Approved refunds may require processing time after the return, cancellation, or refund request has been verified.

Bank or payment-provider processing times may be outside the direct control of CreateLK Shop.

10. Return Shipping

Where the return is caused by a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, CreateLK Shop may arrange or provide instructions for the return depending on the circumstances.

For other return requests, return shipping costs may be the responsibility of the customer unless otherwise stated.

11. Seller-Specific Products

Some products may be supplied by independent sellers or third-party merchants. Product-specific return conditions may therefore apply.

Customers should review the product information and applicable seller conditions before placing an order.

12. Fraudulent or Invalid Claims

CreateLK Shop may investigate refund and return requests that appear fraudulent, misleading, unauthorized, or otherwise invalid.

13. How to Request a Return or Refund

To request a return or refund, please provide:

  • Your name
  • Order number or payment reference
  • Product name
  • Reason for the return or refund
  • Photos or videos where relevant

Please keep your order information, payment receipt, and delivery details until your order has been successfully completed.

Contact Us

If you have questions about a return or refund, please contact CreateLK Shop using the contact information provided on our website.

We will review each request and provide the appropriate next steps.
