DAY 1

Introducing Yourself

Beginner

Learn how to introduce yourself in English. Use simple sentences to tell someone your name, where you are from, where you live, and what you do.

🧠 Vocabulary
Hello = a greeting
Name = what people call you
From = your country or place
Live = the place where you stay
Student = a person who studies
💬 Examples
My name is John.
I am from Sri Lanka.
I live in Colombo.
I am a student.
Nice to meet you.
🗣️ Speaking Practice
Hello. My name is ______.
I am from ______.
I live in ______.
I am a ______.
Nice to meet you.
📝 Daily Quiz — 5 Questions

1. What do you say when you meet someone?

2. Which sentence is correct?

3. Which sentence tells where you are from?

4. What does "Nice to meet you" mean?

5. Choose the correct sentence.
